How we calculate this
Hourly rate = (space base + amenities) × local demand. Half day = 5 h at 60%, full day = 10 h at 40%, overnight = 10 h at 55% of the hourly rate. Each extra pet from the same home adds 60%. Tier bonus: Trusted +5%, Pack Leader +12%. The fair range is ±15% around the suggestion. Parents pay the price plus nothing hidden; you receive 90%.
