Trust & reputation

Reputation is earned stay by stay, on both sides.

Every completed booking closes with a two-way review. Hosts earn a Paw Score and climb tiers; pets earn a Good Guest record that helps them get accepted faster. Reviews only come from paid, completed stays, so they can't be bought or faked.

Fair pricing

Fair to hosts. Clear for pet parents.

We suggest a price for every space from what it really offers: the kind of space, its amenities, the host's tier and local demand. Hosts can go above or below it, and parents always see the full price before booking.

Hosts keep 90% of the stay price. The 10% service fee covers escrow, support and the stay guarantee.
Longer stays cost less per hour, so hosts are paid fairly without parents overpaying.
Late pick-up is billed per 30 minutes, at the agreed hourly rate, never a flat penalty.
